## 2. Background (short)

- Aura's Palmistry guide is a read-only educational section of a commercial personal-planning app. It is unpublished.
- Much of its text reports, in Aura's own words, what historical Sanskrit texts say. For several texts, Aura knew that content only through Kenneth G. Zysk's English translations in *History of Science in South Asia* 13 (2025), which are **CC BY-SA 4.0**. An internal analysis could not rule out that some passages are "Adapted Material".
- **The owner's chosen route (L2-B):** license the complete Palmistry text under CC BY-SA 4.0, keep the Zysk attribution, and keep the interpretations unchanged. The owner accepts, in principle, that third parties may then reuse that text commercially under CC BY-SA.
- **Evidence:** no Aura string shares more than 11 consecutive words with Zysk (a conventional phrase); at most 3 with Wikipedia; no third-party images. This is AI-prepared evidence, not a legal determination.
