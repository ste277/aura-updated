# Aura — 7-Day Pilot: Participant Guide

Thank you for trying Aura for a week. We want to learn whether Aura makes it easier to fit the things that matter into a busy week. **We are testing Aura, not you.** There are no wrong answers and nothing you do can break it.

## What we ask

1. **Open Aura on your phone:** https://aura-updated.vercel.app
   - You can try it before creating an account.
   - If you like, add it to your home screen (Share → *Add to Home Screen* on iPhone, or ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* / *Install app* on Android).
2. **Use it for real things in your own week**, for example exercise, focused work, errands, rest, or time with family or friends. Please use real plans rather than made-up ones.
3. **Use it as much or as little as you naturally would.** If you stop opening it, that tells us something useful too. Please don't force yourself to use it.
4. **Answer three short questions each evening.** They take about two minutes. The owner will send you the link or form.
5. **Have one 20–30 minute conversation at the end of the week.**

That's all. We are deliberately not giving you a tutorial. We want to see what you discover on your own.

## Good to know

- **Sign-in** uses a 6-digit code sent to your email; there is no password. If the code doesn't arrive or you mistype your email, reload the page and start again, and please tell us it happened.
- **Location:** Aura uses your city to calculate timings. Check that it shows your city.
- **Timing guidance** is based on the traditional Indian calendar (Panchangam). You don't need to know anything about Panchangam to take part, and you're free to ignore any suggestion.
- **Tip:** Aura doesn't know your working hours until you tell it. To have suggestions avoid your work time, open **You → Daily Rhythm** and enter your usual work start, end and work days. *(The owner may remove this tip; see the owner notes below.)*
- **Reminders:** Aura **won't** notify you when it's closed. It shows upcoming plans and "starting soon" reminders only when you open the app. If your browser asks for notification permission, you can say yes or no; it doesn't change what you'll see during this pilot.

## Privacy

- Aura stores the activities, plans and check-ins you enter, the same as for any user.
- Aura keeps standard, minimal usage records (for example, which screens were opened and when). No new tracking has been added for this pilot.
- Please don't enter anything you'd consider sensitive, such as health details or other people's private information.
- Your feedback will be summarised without your name. You can leave the pilot at any time, without giving a reason.
- If you'd like your account deleted after the pilot, tell the owner.

## If something goes wrong

Take a screenshot if you can, and send it to the owner with one line describing what you were trying to do. Please don't spend time working around it: the problem itself is the finding.
