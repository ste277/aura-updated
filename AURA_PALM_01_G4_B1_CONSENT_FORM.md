## Part A: Information for participants (keep this page)

**Who is doing this research?** [OWNER: researcher name(s) and organisation / legal entity] ⚖ REVIEW (identity of the data controller)

**What is the research for?** To test whether software running **on a computer controlled by the researcher** can find the main creases on a photo of an open palm. It is engineering research for the Aura app.

**What will happen?**
- We will take **[OWNER: number, e.g. up to 6]** photos of your hands: open palms and the back of the hand, in different lighting, with one or more phone cameras.
- Each photo shows **only the hand**. Please remove rings and watches. We avoid faces, tattoos, nail art and anything else that could identify you. If any appears, we'll retake the photo or crop it out.
- It takes about **[OWNER: minutes]** minutes.

**Can a palm photo identify me?** Possibly. Palm creases and palm prints can be unique to a person and may count as **biometric or personal information** in some laws. This research **does not try to identify anyone** or match photos to people, but we treat the photos as sensitive. ⚖ REVIEW (biometric-data rules, e.g. GDPR Art. 9, India DPDP Act, US state biometric laws such as Illinois BIPA)

**What are the photos used for?**
- **Development:** a researcher may use some photos to adjust the settings of the detection software.
- **Evaluation:** the other photos are used only to measure accuracy.
- The photos are **not** used to train AI models, are **not** processed by cloud or online AI services, and are **not** sent to the app's servers.
- Photos stay on **[OWNER: storage location description, e.g. an encrypted drive on the researcher's computer]**.

**Who can see the photos?** Only: **[OWNER: named roles, e.g. the researcher and up to two annotators]**. Annotators draw lines on the creases on that computer. Nobody else.

**Will results be published?** Only **aggregate numbers** (for example, "the software found 80% of creases") may appear in **[OWNER: internal reports only / public reports]**. **Your photos will never be published** or shown outside the access list above.

**What information is kept with the photos?**
- A **code** (e.g. P07-L-01), which hand, the lighting conditions and the phone model.
- **Not your name.** Location data and other hidden photo metadata are **removed** before storage.
- **Optional, self-reported:** you may choose to tell us your skin-tone group (a three-group scale), only to check the software works fairly for everyone. You can skip this. **We won't guess it from your photo.** [OWNER: keep or remove this question] ⚖ REVIEW (sensitive/special-category data)

**How long are photos kept?** Until **[OWNER: date or event]**, after which they are securely deleted. A deletion record (codes and date, no photos) is kept.

**Can I change my mind?** Yes. Taking part is voluntary. You can withdraw **at any time, without giving a reason**, by contacting **[OWNER: contact method]**, quoting your participant code. We will delete your photos and annotations within **[OWNER: number] days** and confirm. Aggregate numbers already calculated from many photos can't be recalculated without your photos removed, but will be **[OWNER: recalculated / left as is]** for future reports.

**Who can I contact?** [OWNER: contact for questions and complaints] · ⚖ REVIEW (data-protection contact or regulator details if required)

**Eligibility:** 18 or older.
