Aura needs an updated WebView

Your device's web component, Android System WebView, is older than the version Aura requires, so Aura can't open on this device yet.

How to fix this

Please update Android System WebView through your device's app store, or check for a device software update. After updating, reopen Aura.

Changing your internet connection won't fix this. If your device no longer receives WebView updates, it may not be compatible with this version of Aura.
