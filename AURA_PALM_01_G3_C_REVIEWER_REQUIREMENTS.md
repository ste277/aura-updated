# AURA-PALM-01-G3-C: Reviewer C (External Copyright Counsel) Requirements

**Status:** External counsel is approved as the Reviewer C pathway, but **no one is selected, contacted or appointed**. Nothing below names a lawyer, fee or contact. All assignment fields are for the product owner to complete.

## 1. Scope of the role

Reviewer C gives a **written legal opinion** on:
- C1 (L1): adaptation and ShareAlike;
- C2 (L2): consequences;
- C3 (N3): AI-generated text and drawings;
- C4: India and US coverage;
- optionally C5 (L3, L4, L6).

See `AURA_PALM_01_G3_C_COUNSEL_BRIEF.md`.

Reviewer C is **not** responsible for scholarly accuracy (Reviewer A) or editorial and cultural approval (Reviewer B). Counsel's opinion does not by itself close any L-finding: each closes only when its documented evidence is accepted and recorded.

## 1A. Licensing finding closure and publication-blocker status

These classifications are preserved from G3-L1 (`AURA_PALM_01_G3_L1_LICENSING_ACTIONS.md`). Counsel's opinion is evidence; a finding closes only when its closure evidence is accepted and recorded.

| Finding | G3-L1 status | Primary closer | **Fallback closer if counsel declines or isn't asked** | Closure evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1: adaptation / ShareAlike | **Publication blocker (P1)** | External counsel | None. Counsel is required (Path B) | Written opinion |
| L2: consequences | **P1, conditional on L1** | Counsel (legal) + product owner (decision) | None for the legal part | Opinion + owner decision record |
| N3: AI-generated content | **P1 until counsel says it doesn't block** | External counsel | None | Written note |
| L3: attribution | **P1** | Counsel (optional opinion, brief C5) | **Internal licensing reviewer** with CC expertise (audit §14) | Recorded L3 decision |
| L4: modification notice | **P1** | Counsel (optional) | **Internal licensing reviewer** | Recorded L4 decision (+ wording) |
| L6: drawing originality | **P1** | Counsel (optional) | **Internal licensing reviewer** (visual comparison) | Recorded comparison |
| L5: Iyer public domain | P2, not blocking | Internal licensing reviewer | — | Authority record or recorded acceptance |
| L7: terms / locators | P2, not blocking | Internal licensing reviewer | — | Recorded acceptance |
| L8: name / icons | P2, not blocking | Internal licensing reviewer | — | Recorded acceptance |

If counsel doesn't cover L3, L4 and L6, the product owner must designate the internal licensing reviewer before publication is considered. Governance findings GF-1 to GF-3 (MVP decisions, PR #338 authorization, M1/M2 records) are separate publication gates. They stay **open** and are tracked in `AURA_PALM_01_G3_DISTRIBUTION/GOVERNANCE_FINDINGS_TRACKER.md`.
