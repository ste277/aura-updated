# AURA-PALM-01-G4-B2: Privacy Recommendations

**Not legal advice or a legal determination.** Each item separates an **engineering recommendation** (what the research setup can do) from **⚖ legal review** (what a qualified privacy/legal adviser must decide).

| # | Topic | Engineering recommendation | ⚖ Requires qualified legal review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Applicable jurisdictions** | Recruit participants from **one known jurisdiction** for the first round, and record it on the consent record (e.g. "participant resides in: ___"). Avoid participants from regions with strict biometric statutes until reviewed (e.g. US states with biometric-specific laws such as Illinois, Texas, Washington) | Which laws apply (participant residence, researcher location, entity); whether palm photos + landmark extraction are "biometric identifiers", "hand geometry" or special-category data; DPDP Act (India) notice/consent duties; GDPR/UK GDPR if any EU/UK participants |
| 2 | **Consent formalities and PIA** | Written consent (Part B), versioned; a copy for the participant; a short internal **privacy impact assessment** using the G4-B1 risk register as its basis | Whether written vs verbal suffices; required notice content and language; whether a formal DPIA or ethics/IRB-style review is required or advisable |
| 3 | **Responsible entity and contact** | Name one accountable person and one withdrawal channel (e.g. a dedicated email address not used for anything else) | Which legal entity is the controller; whether a data-protection contact or representative is required |
| 4 | **Retention and withdrawal** | **Default proposal:** retain until G4 research concludes or **6 months after the first capture, whichever is earlier**; withdrawal deletion within **7 days**; deletion log | Whether these periods meet legal requirements (some laws require a published retention schedule and maximum periods) |
| 5 | **Optional skin-tone self-report** | **Recommended: keep it optional, self-reported, 3 groups, with "prefer not to say"**; never inferred. Keep the frozen criteria; report C7 by skin tone as "not measurable" if fewer than 6 self-reported images per group | Whether skin tone counts as sensitive/special-category data needing explicit consent or a stronger legal basis |
| 6 | **Metadata stripping** | **ImageMagick** (`magick -auto-orient -strip`), already installed; HEIC via `sips` inside the encrypted volume; `verify_clean.sh` must print CLEAN for every image (metadata tool evaluation) | None beyond confirming metadata removal is adequate de-identification support |
| 7 | **Encrypted backup and recovery** | **Default: no backup** (re-collectable research data; fewer copies). If wanted: one encrypted offline copy, logged, deleted with the dataset | Whether retention and deletion obligations reach backups (assume yes) |
| 8 | **Code-to-person key** | **Default: no key.** Participants keep their own code for withdrawal (on their copy of the form). If a key is needed (e.g. to contact participants), keep it only in the CONSENT volume (passphrase B) | Whether withdrawal-by-code without a key is acceptable; access-right handling |
| 9 | **AI assistant access** | Enforce S-1 to S-8 (AI access safeguards): no AI views images or image-rendering pages; AI receives aggregate, coordinate-free results only | Whether any AI-tool use counts as processing by a third party (it shouldn't occur under S-1 to S-8); disclosure in the consent text ("no cloud AI services") is already included |

**Overall:** engineering safeguards are ready. **Items 1–5 and 8 have legal dimensions that are unresolved**, and need review, or a documented owner risk acceptance, before collection.
