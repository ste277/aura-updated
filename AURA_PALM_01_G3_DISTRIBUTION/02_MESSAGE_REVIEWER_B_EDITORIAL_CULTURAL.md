**What the guide is.** The guide is read-only. Users read an introduction, then select one of four lines on an original schematic drawing of a hand, or one of five traditional marks, to read how historical Indian texts interpreted it. It does not read the user's hand, use photos, personalise anything or make predictions. Most users are general adults in India and the Indian diaspora. Many know palmistry culturally but have no textual training.

**Its status.** The guide is **not approved for publication** and is hidden from all users. 4. Whether the text avoids deterministic or misleading claims about health, lifespan, relationships, children or money.
