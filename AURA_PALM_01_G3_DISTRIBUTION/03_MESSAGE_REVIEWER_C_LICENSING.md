> Kenneth G. Zysk, "Early Indian Palmistry. Śārdūlakarṇāvadāna: Pāṇilekhā", *History of Science in South Asia* 13 (2025): 240–275, DOI 10.18732/hssa128. Licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
